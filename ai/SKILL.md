# Intent interview

1. Read the root `AGENTS.md` and, if it exists, the root `intent.md` before starting. Do not require a separate glossary or template.
2. Conduct requirements discovery in these six chronological phases:
   - Core Value Proposition & Target Audience
   - Core User Persona Workflows
   - Platforms & Tech Stack
   - Non-Functional Requirements
   - Key Integrations
   - Constraints & Scope
3. Ask only one question at a time and wait for the stakeholder's answer. Ask a focused follow-up when an answer is unclear. Do not impose a fixed question limit; continue until the phase is sufficiently understood or the stakeholder asks to stop.
4. Summarize each completed phase and confirm the summary before proceeding. Distinguish confirmed answers, preferences, assumptions, and unresolved questions. Never invent business requirements or present undecided technology choices as decisions.
5. After discovery, create or update the root `intent.md`. Preserve its established structure and headings:
   - Product Intent
   - Project
   - Product purpose
   - Target users
   - Intended workflows, with role-specific subsections
   - Order information and behavior
   - Platform and technology
   - Quality expectations
   - MVP scope
   - Constraints
   - Open questions
6. Keep the content concise and faithful to stakeholder answers; paraphrase clearly rather than implying verbatim quotations. Put unresolved or unanswered items under `Open questions`. Do not add a separate approval/status section unless the stakeholder requests it.
